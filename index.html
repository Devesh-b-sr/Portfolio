THE CORE PILLARS OF KULDHARA

Shadows of Kuldhara is structured around an uncompromising psychological morality engine where the player's internal state directly mutates both combat mechanics and narrative reality. Rather than simple branching dialogue trees, the core architecture balances two opposing subconscious forces: the Mother Hallucination (a path of discipline, non-lethal precision, stealth, and moral redemption) versus the Brother Hallucination (an intoxicating spiral of relentless fury, heavy greatsword brutality, and scorched-earth vengeance). Every assassination target and environmental encounter acts as a testing ground for Dev's fractured psyche, directly dictating boss fight resolutions, environmental reactivity, and the ultimate fate of Kuldhara.

01 // PSYCHOLOGICAL DUALITY Real-time hallucination debates altering audio/visual perception and moral branching.
02 // ADAPTIVE ARSENAL Dual light daggers & bow mechanics shifting into a heavy execution greatsword.
03 // BOSS REACTION LOGIC Encounter outcomes (disarm vs. execute) that permanently reshape village factions.
